# 🎉 New NGIAB Research Paper Published!

We're excited to announce that our research on **NextGen In A Box (NGIAB)** has been published in a peer-reviewed journal! This milestone represents a significant achievement for the CIROH community and our collaborative efforts in advancing water prediction capabilities.

## 📄 Publication Details

The paper has been published in **Environmental Modelling & Software**, a leading journal in computational environmental science. This publication showcases the innovative work our team has been developing to make NextGen water prediction models more accessible and deployable.

**🔗 [Read the full paper here](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1364815225003500)**

## 🌟 What This Means for the Community

This publication represents:

- **Validation** of NGIAB's scientific approach and methodology
- **Recognition** from the broader scientific community
- **Documentation** of best practices for containerized hydrological modeling
- **Foundation** for future research and development

The peer-review process has helped refine our approach and will guide future development of NGIAB. The insights gained during this research will continue to improve:

- Model containerization strategies
- Deployment methodologies
- Community adoption practices
- Technical documentation
